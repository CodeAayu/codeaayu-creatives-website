About Me

Turning moments into timeless visual stories

I'm Aayush, a passionate photographer based in Bangalore with a love for capturing authentic moments. I specialize in travel photography, portrait photography, and creative content creation.

My journey started at IIT(ISM) Dhanbad pursuing Integrated MTech in Mathematics and Computing. What began as a hobby evolved into a full-fledged passion for visual storytelling.

Currently working as a Software Engineer at udaan.com, I blend my technical expertise with artistic vision to create images that resonate.
