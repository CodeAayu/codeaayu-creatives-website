Operating model

A simple production pipeline.

The process is built like a small product cycle: clarify the use case, build the visual spec, shoot with intent, and deliver a usable set.

01

Brief

Goal, audience, references, channels, location, and constraints become the creative spec.

02

Capture

Shot lists stay flexible, but light, composition, and story intent are controlled on location.

03

Grade

Color and contrast are tuned as a system, so the final set feels coherent across surfaces.

04

Ship

Exports are packaged for web, social, print, portfolio, and campaign use without extra friction.
