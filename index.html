01 / The experience

Less posing.
More living.

A shoot with me is not a performance. It is a place where the music is good, the directions are easy, and being yourself is enough.

I bring the structure. You bring your people, your strange little jokes, your favourite clothes, your nervous energy. Somewhere between those things, we make frames that still feel alive years later.
