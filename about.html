My Story

The Journey So Far

My journey began at IIT(ISM) Dhanbad, where I pursued an Integrated MTech in Mathematics and Computing. What started as a simple hobby of capturing moments during college events evolved into a full-fledged passion for visual storytelling that would shape my creative identity.

As a travel and portrait photographer, I'm drawn to capturing authentic moments and telling stories through my lens. Whether it's candid expressions, golden hour landscapes, or intimate portrait sessions, I strive to create images that resonate with viewers and evoke emotion.

My background in technology and current role as a Software Engineer at udaan.com has given me a unique perspective on the intersection of creativity and technology. This dual experience helps me approach visual storytelling with both artistic intuition and technical precision.

Beyond photography, I'm passionate about writing and content creation. Through my articles on Medium, I share insights about creativity, personal growth, travel experiences, and technology. I believe in building a community of fellow creatives and learning from each other's journeys.
