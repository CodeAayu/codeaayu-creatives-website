Shoot protocol

How I approach a project.

The process is intentionally simple: understand what the images need to do, create a visual direction, capture with room for discovery, and deliver an organized set.

01

Brief the use case

Audience, channel, location, timeline, references, and delivery format become the creative constraint.

02

Build the visual map

Shot priorities are planned before the shoot, but the frame stays responsive to weather, mood, and movement.

03

Deliver as a set

Edits are graded for consistency and exported for actual use across web, social, print, and campaign surfaces.
